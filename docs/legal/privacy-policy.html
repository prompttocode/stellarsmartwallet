2. Information we collect and why

Account and sign-in information

Email address, Privy user ID, linked sign-in identifiers and profile information made available through your selected sign-in method. You can sign in using email, Google or Apple. Apple may supply a relay email address if you choose Hide My Email. We use this information to authenticate you, associate your wallets with your account and restore access. Your Google or Apple password is not submitted to our wallet backend.

Wallet and transaction information

Wallet IDs, public addresses, wallet labels, selected network, balances, assets, trustlines, sender/recipient addresses, amounts, fees, transaction hashes, memos, timestamps, transaction status and transaction payloads. We use these to display your wallet, obtain swap quotes, prepare and submit transactions, and maintain history.

Identity verification information

When you submit identity verification, we receive photographs of the front and back of your Vietnamese identity card (CCCD), your account email and any phone number you provide. The images may contain your portrait, signature or printed fingerprint information, as present on the document.

We store the full identity number, name, date of birth, sex, nationality, home/residential address, contact information, verification-service user ID and status, and the full structured response returned by our identity service. Additional returned fields, such as document expiry and record timestamps, may be retained in that response. Image references are retained when returned by the service. Masking information in the app does not mean the backend stores only a masked version.

These records support identity checks and VND account/order handling. The wallet backend forwards your images to our Seerbot identity service. Its integration documentation describes persistent image storage and OCR processing; see Section 4 for the production details still requiring confirmation.

Bank information and VND orders

Bank identifier/name, full bank account number, account holder name, account type, saved/default payment method, and buy/withdrawal order details. Order records may include crypto and VND amounts, exchange rates, fees, wallet addresses, payment references, payment instructions, settlement details, timestamps and status. We use them to create orders, receive or make payments, reconcile results and assist with disputes. We do not ask for your internet-banking password or banking OTP in this flow.

Preferences, support and operational records

Selected currency/network, active wallet, favorite assets, cached balances/history, WalletConnect sessions, ratings, feedback category/message, app version and related account/wallet identifiers. Our backend also records technical events, request IDs, errors, response metadata and transaction/order references for operation and troubleshooting. Some records can be linked to your account.

Our infrastructure and external services receive network information such as IP address, request headers, device/OS/browser information and timestamps when contacted. The wallet/KYC flow does not request precise GPS location; a residential address read from your CCCD is different from live device location.

3. Wallet keys and device authentication

Privy supplies embedded-wallet infrastructure for wallets created through that flow. Signing and recovery use Privy services and the authorizations configured for the wallet. The mobile app offers a secure hosted recovery/export flow.

Imported wallets are different: if you use the current Import wallet flow, the private key you enter is sent to our backend, encrypted and stored with the wallet record. The backend can decrypt it to sign transactions in that flow. This is not a device-only key-storage model, and we do not claim that all wallet keys are held exclusively on your phone or exclusively by Privy.

Face ID/Touch ID/device biometric prompts return an authentication result to the app. The app does not receive the device's biometric templates. This is separate from any portrait or fingerprint image visible on a CCCD that you choose to upload.

5. Public blockchain information

Confirmed Stellar transactions and public wallet activity can be viewed, copied and analyzed by anyone. Deleting your OrbitWallet account cannot erase blockchain records or copies independently held by network participants or explorers. Do not put personal identity information into transaction memos.

6. Local storage, camera and cookies

The app stores preferences and cached account/wallet data on your device. Supported sensitive authentication tokens use device secure storage. Hosted sign-in/recovery pages and their providers may use cookies or similar storage to maintain sessions.

Camera permission is used for QR scanning and photographing your identity document. CCCD capture creates temporary files for preview and compression before submission; it does not upload your entire photo library. You may revoke Camera access in device settings, but camera-based verification and scanning will then be unavailable.

Temporary image cleanup is performed during supported capture/reset flows. It is not a guarantee that every temporary file, cache, backup or service copy disappears immediately after submission or after an interrupted session.

7. Retention and account deletion

You can start account deletion in Settings. The current deletion flow removes your account-linked KYC, bank methods, VND-order copies, favorites, feedback and exchange-profile records from the wallet D1 database and requests deletion of your Privy user. Apple authorization revocation is attempted when required with the necessary authorization information.

This action does not automatically delete all Seerbot records, OCR/image-storage copies, infrastructure logs or backups. It also does not erase blockchain history or necessarily remove backend transaction/temporary-preparation records. Contact [PUBLIC CONTACT EMAIL] to request review of remaining personal information. Keep a secure recovery backup and resolve outstanding orders before deleting your account.
